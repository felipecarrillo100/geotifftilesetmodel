The whole look is one JSON-serialisable object, passed to model.setStyle(). Edit and apply.
                    Save the current layer as a manifest…

                
                

                    Apply
                    
                

                

            

            
                

                    Every tile is read with HTTP range requests from the original COGs (Sentinel-2 keeps each band in its own file, at 10, 20 or 60 m).
                    The bands are resampled onto one tile grid, decoded values are cached, and the style is applied on the CPU with lookup tables:
                    band math is compiled into a tight loop (no eval of user text: a whitelisted grammar), stretches come from
                    histograms of an overview, colours from 1024-entry LUTs. LuciadRIA then drapes the RGBA tiles on the 3D globe.
                

                
Changing style never fetches data again: watch the cache hits in the top-right panel.
